1. How do I approach designing software?

This project helped me improve at breaking a larger idea into smaller, manageable parts. For my 3D scene, I started by identifying basic shapes and then considered their size, position, textures, and lighting. I followed a gradual design process by adding features throughout each milestone and testing them along the way. I can use this same approach in future projects by planning first, developing one feature at a time, and making adjustments as needed.

2. How do I approach developing programs?

I learned to develop and test my program in smaller stages instead of trying to complete everything at once. Iteration was important because I frequently had to adjust object positions, textures, lighting, and other settings after seeing the results. Throughout the milestones, I became more organized and gained a better understanding of how the different parts of the program work together. This made it easier to troubleshoot problems and complete the final scene.

3. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computational graphics helped strengthen my skills with C++, transformations, user input, debugging, and organizing code into reusable functions. These skills will be useful in future computer science courses and larger programming projects. Professionally, the problem-solving, testing, and debugging skills I developed can apply to many areas of software development, even if I do not work specifically with computer graphics.
